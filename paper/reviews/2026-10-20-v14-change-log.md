## 2. New analyses (approved by the author on 2026-10-20)

1. Common explained records. Random halves (`p9_common_records.py`): gap on common records 0.123–0.130 for XGBoost (0.129 on
   own records) and 0.134–0.147 for Random Forest (0.186), positive in all 11 repeats (about 21–28% of the RF gap from the
   explained records). Row blocks (`designs.py` with `EXPLAIN_OTHER=1`): calibrated 0.063–0.072 (XGB, own 0.074) and
   0.107–0.119 (RF, own 0.153); first-vs-second half 0.031–0.032 (XGB, own 0.033) and
   0.061–0.071 (RF, own 0.093); positive in 10–11 of 11 repeats. Every refit reproduces the stored design exactly.
2. Grouped permutation null, two versions (`designs.py nullstrat_*` flow-level, `nullgroup_*` group-level), five settings
   (clusters ~10, ~35, calibrated; row blocks calibrated, first-vs-second half). Row-block nulls near zero in both versions
   (-0.022 to 0.023); real row-block gaps exceed the group-level null by 0.069 and 0.035 (XGB) and
   0.130 and 0.111 (RF). For clusters the flow-level null is negative (-0.095 to -0.009) and the group-level null
   positive (0.040 to 0.265); the real cluster gaps lie between them in every setting, so no neutral null exists for clusters.
   Superseded versions, kept for the record and not used: `null_*` (halves could differ in Non-VPN/VPN source mix, per-class
   difference up to 0.99, `p12_null_origin_check.json`) and the first calibrated runs of the two null versions (`*_unscaled`,
   groups re-cut to 0.3–0.7 of capture size; corrected by scaling the group count to the pseudo-context's share of each file).
Text changed for these results: I (contributions), IV-D, VI-C (two new paragraphs, Table VII rows and note), X-A, X-E, XI,
XII, abstract (last sentence), Table XV (three rows), Fig. 2.
