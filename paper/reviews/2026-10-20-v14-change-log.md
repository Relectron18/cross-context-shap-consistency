## 2. New analyses (approved by the author on 2026-10-20)

1. Common explained records (`p9_common_records.py`): every random-half model explained on both contexts' records.
   Gap on common records 0.123–0.130 for XGBoost (0.129 on own records) and 0.134–0.147 for Random Forest (0.186), positive
   in all 11 repeats; about 21–28% of the Random Forest gap comes from the explained records. Primary models on common
   records: cross-context ρ 0.863–0.883 (RF), 0.771–0.787 (XGB).
2. Grouped permutation null, two versions (`designs.py nullstrat_*`, `nullgroup_*`): row-block nulls near zero in both
   (−0.022 to 0.032); real row-block gaps exceed the group-level null by 0.035–0.063 (XGB) and 0.111–0.121 (RF). For
   clusters the flow-level null is negative (−0.064 to −0.009) and the group-level null positive (0.041 to 0.265), and the
   real cluster gaps lie between them; no neutral null exists for clusters.
   A first version (`null_*`) was superseded before use: its halves differed in Non-VPN/VPN source mix (per-class
   difference up to 0.99, `p12_null_origin_check.json`).
Text changed for these results: I (contributions), IV-D, VI-C (two new paragraphs, Table VII rows, Table VII note), X-A,
X-E, XI, XII, abstract (last sentence), Fig. 2.
